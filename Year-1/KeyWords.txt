Personal fable - the belief that an individual is unique and special, leading to feelings of invulnerability
MegaCognition - awareness and understanding of one's own thought processes.
Incubation effect - a psychology phenomenon where taking a break from a problem leads to a creative solution when you return to it later
Empiricist - a philosophical belief that states your knowledge of the world is based on your experiences, particularly your sensory experiences
Crystallized Intelligence - the accumulated knowledge, skills, and facts a person has acquired throughout their life via education and experience
fluid intelligence - the ability to reason, solve problems, and think abstractly in new and unfamiliar situations without relying on prior knowledge
empirical - based on experiments and practical experience, not on ideas
prejudice - a strong unreasonable feeling of not liking or trusting somebody/something, especially when it is based on their race, religion or sex
persuasion - the act of persuading somebody to do something or to believe something
stereotypes - a fixed idea about a particular type of person or thing, which is often not true in reality
Conformity - the act of changing one's behavior, beliefs, or attitudes to match those of a group or social norms
Altruism - the selfless concern for the well-being of others, even at a cost to oneself
deception - making somebody believe or being made to believe something that is not true
introspection - the process of thinking deeply and carefully to examine your own ideas, feelings, etc.
predisposition - a condition that makes somebody/something likely to behave in a particular way or suffer from a medical condition
narcissism - the habit of admiring yourself too much, especially your appearance
Impulsivity - the tendency to act quickly without thinking about the potential consequences, often leading to actions that are risky, poorly conceived, or inappropriate
inhibition - a shy or nervous feeling that stops you from saying or doing what you really want
Procrastination - the act of unnecessarily delaying or postponing tasks, despite knowing there may be negative consequences
